hen I first started building this portfolio, it began as something practical: a place to show the work. But somewhere along the way, it became something more — a reflection of how I actually think, and why the combination of economics and fashion media was never an accident.

I grew up obsessing over two things that most people treat as opposites: numbers and narrative. Spreadsheets and aesthetics. Strategy and story. My degree at SMU gave me the language for both — and the last four years gave me the proof that they belong together.

What you'll find here is the result of that belief in action. A B2B email campaign that earned a 39% open rate because the copy understood the audience. A luxury PR internship in London that taught me how reputation is built one placement at a time. A brand operations project that proved beautiful marketing falls apart without a clean backend. An editorial platform that reached 450,000 people because it respected what they actually wanted to see.

I use AI as part of my daily workflow — not as a shortcut, but as a tool for thinking faster without losing the craft. This is not a portfolio of everything I've done. It's a portfolio of how I work: curious, rigorous, and always asking whether the strategy and the story are saying the same thing.

With intention, Isabella Stryker

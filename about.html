A Short Biography

I'm Bella Stryker, a brand strategist based in Dallas and available to relocate. My work sits at the intersection of brand positioning, content systems, and growth — shaped by a background in both economics and fashion media at Southern Methodist University.

I've worked across early-stage and established brands, building systems that support both creative output and operational scale. This includes developing inventory infrastructure for a luxury fashion brand, leading digital and editorial strategy for SMU's independent fashion publication, and contributing to client placement and media strategy at ModusBPCM in London.

I'm most interested in the work behind the work — the systems, structures, and decisions that allow brands to grow in a way that's both intentional and scalable.

I value efficiency, problem-solving, and loving what I do.

When I'm Not Working

When I close my laptop, I'm usually in the kitchen. I love cooking long, complex dinners at home — the kind that take all afternoon and involve way too many dishes — and I've been deep in the sourdough rabbit hole for a while now. It's genuinely the most meditative thing I do.

I'm a Lagree Pilates regular and I hike whenever I can get to a trail. I studied abroad in London twice during college, which gave me the travel bug in a permanent way — I'm always either planning a trip or recovering from one.

I've been obsessed with James Bond movies since I was little, and adventure films in general are my comfort watch. And I'm a huge restaurant person — always trying somewhere new. If you want proof, find me on Beli — I'm @isabellastryker.

A Short Biography

A strategist, an editor, a reader of the numbers.

I'm a brand strategist and editorial director based in Dallas and available to relocate. My work sits at the intersection of luxury positioning, content systems, and commercial analysis — shaped equally by a Bachelor of Science in Economics and a Bachelor of Arts in Fashion Media from Southern Methodist University.

I've built brand foundations for a luxury handbag atelier, redesigned the editorial platform of SMU's independent fashion publication, launched acquisition programs for a Boston-based marketplace, and placed clients across UK architecture and design press during a term at ModusBPCM in London.

I'm drawn to work that rewards precision: brand voice documents, inventory analyses, editorial calendars, investor-facing narratives. The common thread is structure — the sense that a well-built framework should be quietly visible underneath anything I make.

Read case study
Role
Brand Strategy Intern — led a full operational and growth-focused infrastructure rebuild across inventory, Shopify optimization, and marketing alignment.
The Problem
A growing luxury fashion brand with strong product-market fit was operating on fragmented inventory systems and reactive marketing. Shopify lacked structure, tracking was manual, and stock movement wasn't aligned with promotion strategy.
What I Did
  • Cleaned and optimized the Shopify backend — product organization, tagging, categorization.
  • Built a fully automated inventory spreadsheet synced with Shopify.
  • Implemented an ABC classification system to prioritize high-revenue SKUs and guide merchandising.
  • Created a marketing strategy aligned to inventory velocity — push A-items, move B-items, strategically discount C-items.
  • Reorganized the physical inventory room and built a recurring quarterly ABC workflow.
  • Contributed to paid ad creative and performance optimization, plus an updated media kit for brand partnerships.
Impact
~30–40% sell-through of targeted aging stock within weeks of campaign launch.
Operational clarity across 100% of SKUs. Quarterly ABC workflow adopted as an ongoing system.
Tools
Shopify · GA4 · Excel · Inventory Forecasting Models · Paid Meta Ads · Notion

Full-Scope Partnership

House of Arella

The Challenge

The brand needed cohesion across its entire marketing ecosystem — website, social media, packaging, and brand materials were disconnected and didn't reflect the luxury positioning the founder envisioned.

The Strategy

Conducting a full deep dive into all marketing materials to prioritize cohesion across the business. This includes a complete website update and platform migration to Shopify, a branding and packaging materials review, building a content workflow for ongoing social media management, and delivering foundational strategy documents.

The Results

• Unified brand identity across all touchpoints
• Website redesign on modern e-commerce platform
• Streamlined content production workflow
• Clear positioning and messaging framework

Operations · Systems

Sai Sankoh

The Challenge

The founder was handling everything manually — marketing, operations, and client communication were disconnected and unsustainable as the business scaled.

The Strategy

Built integrated workflow automations connecting marketing campaigns to operational processes. This freed the founder to focus on design and product while systems managed the operational overhead.

The Results

• 20+ hours saved per week
• Improved customer communication
• Scalable operational processes
• Founder focused on core business

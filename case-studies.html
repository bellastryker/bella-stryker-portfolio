01 ReSupply
B2B Marketing Growth Email
39% Avg. Email Open Rate
2 New Paid Channels Launched
B2B Realtor-Focused Campaign
The Brief

ReSupply is a B2B platform helping realtors streamline the process of connecting their clients with donation and junk removal services during home transitions. The challenge: build brand awareness and generate qualified leads among a skeptical, busy professional audience — realtors — who are pitched constantly.

As Marketing & Sales Intern, I was tasked with building a go-to-market marketing campaign from the ground up, identifying the right channels, crafting the messaging, and executing across email and paid media.

The Approach

I started with audience research — analyzing how realtors consume content, what objections they raise, and what language resonates. The insight: realtors care about speed, client experience, and anything that makes them look more thoughtful. ReSupply solves all three, so the campaign leaned into that.

I developed the full email marketing funnel: audience segmentation, subject line testing, copywriting, and sequence design. I also worked with the team to identify and launch two new paid advertising channels, developing the creative briefs and channel strategy for each.

The Result

The email campaign achieved a 39% average open rate — well above the 20–25% industry benchmark for B2B marketing. The campaign drove measurable lead pipeline growth and laid the foundation for two new paid channels that expanded ReSupply's reach beyond organic and referral traffic.

What I Did
  • Built full-funnel B2B email marketing campaign targeting realtors
  • Developed audience segmentation and messaging strategy
  • Wrote all email copy, subject lines, and CTAs
  • Designed A/B testing framework for subject lines and content
  • Developed channel strategy and creative briefs for two new paid channels
  • Tracked and reported on campaign KPIs weekly
02 Sai Sankoh
Brand Strategy Operations Shopify
100% Inventory Reconciled
1 Marketing Kit Delivered
Shopify Automation System Built
The Brief

Sai Sankoh is an emerging luxury fashion brand navigating the operational complexity of rapid growth. When I came on as a Brand Strategy & Operations intern, the core challenge was clear: the brand's marketing was disconnected from its actual inventory, leading to wasted ad spend and customer experience gaps.

The ask was both operational and strategic — fix the backend, then align the marketing to it.

The Approach

I led a full-scale inventory overhaul using Shopify's automation tools — rebuilding the product catalog, standardizing SKUs, and implementing tracking workflows so the team could see in real time what was available, what was low, and what was sold out. This wasn't just an operations project — it was the foundation for smarter marketing.

Once inventory was clean, I developed a strategic marketing kit that aligned advertising efforts directly to inventory data: what to promote, when, and how aggressively, based on stock levels and sell-through rates.

The Result

The inventory system gave the brand clarity it hadn't had before — and the marketing kit gave the team a framework to act on it. Campaigns could now be planned and adjusted based on actual product availability, reducing the risk of promoting out-of-stock items and improving conversion efficiency.

What I Did
  • Led full inventory overhaul via Shopify automation tools
  • Rebuilt product catalog with standardized SKU structure
  • Created real-time inventory tracking workflows
  • Developed strategic marketing kit linking ad strategy to inventory data
  • Produced brand content aligned with product launch calendar
  • Supported brand voice development across digital channels
03 ModusBPCM
PR Luxury London
4+ Press Placements Secured
London International Market
Luxury Design & Fashion Clients
The Brief

ModusBPCM is a boutique PR agency in London representing a portfolio of high-end fashion and design clients. As a PR intern in their London office, I worked across multiple client accounts simultaneously — building media relationships, pitching press coverage, and supporting influencer campaigns in one of the world's most competitive luxury markets.

The Approach

Luxury PR is a precision game — the right journalist, the right angle, the right moment. My work centered on building and maintaining targeted media lists tailored to each client's positioning and audience. I researched editorial calendars, identified relevant coverage opportunities, and crafted pitches that fit the media landscape rather than fighting it.

I also supported influencer campaigns — coordinating product sends, tracking coverage, and building relationships with content creators aligned with each client's aesthetic and brand values.

The Result

Secured press placements for luxury design and fashion clients in London publications and outlets. The experience gave me an inside view of how high-end brands build and protect their reputation — the craft of placement, timing, and relationship management that makes PR work in competitive markets.

What I Did
  • Built targeted media lists for luxury fashion and design clients
  • Researched and pitched press coverage opportunities
  • Secured placements in London-based publications
  • Coordinated influencer outreach and product gifting campaigns
  • Tracked media coverage and compiled client reports
  • Assisted with event coordination and showroom support
04 SMU LOOK
Editorial Content Social Media
450K+ Organic Reach
Viral Pi Beta Phi Content
Multi Platform Strategy
The Brief

SMU LOOK is the fashion and lifestyle publication of Southern Methodist University. As Digital Director, I led the content and social strategy across all platforms — from editorial planning and shoot direction to distribution, growth, and performance analysis.

Alongside this role, I led social media for Pi Beta Phi at SMU, creating and managing content that connected the chapter with a broader audience.

The Approach

At LOOK, I built a cross-platform content calendar that balanced editorial storytelling with platform-native formats — knowing that what performs on TikTok is different from what drives engagement on Instagram or traffic from Pinterest. Every piece of content started with a question: who is this for, where will they find it, and why will they share it?

For Pi Beta Phi, I identified trending content formats early, developed a consistent visual identity, and leaned into authentic storytelling over polished production — which drove the engagement that ultimately powered the viral reach.

The Result

Content produced for Pi Beta Phi reached 450K+ organically — a number that came from strategy, not luck. Understanding the algorithm, the audience, and the moment made the difference. At SMU LOOK, I grew the publication's digital footprint and built out editorial and production processes that outlasted my tenure.

What I Did
  • Led cross-platform content strategy as Digital Director of SMU LOOK
  • Directed editorial calendar planning and content production
  • Managed social media presence across Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest
  • Created viral content reaching 450K+ organic views for Pi Beta Phi
  • Built and standardized visual identity guidelines
  • Analyzed performance data to iterate content strategy

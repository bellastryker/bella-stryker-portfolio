Working With Me

Every engagement starts with a full deep dive into your marketing ecosystem. I audit everything — website, brand materials, packaging, social presence, workflows — to identify where cohesion breaks down and where opportunity lives.

The Deep Dive

  • Full audit of all marketing materials and brand touchpoints

Brand brief document, social media metrics spreadsheet, strategic roadmap

Refreshed website, updated brand materials, content calendar, workflow automations

Ongoing Partnership

About Me

Where strategy meets story.

I'm Isabella — a marketer finishing a double degree at SMU in Economics with Financial Applications and Fashion Media. The combination isn't accidental. It's how I think.

Over the past two years, I've built experience across B2B growth marketing, luxury fashion PR, and editorial media — writing campaigns, building operational systems, and producing content that actually moves numbers. A 39% email open rate at ReSupply. Press placements for luxury clients in London. 450K+ organic reach for Pi Beta Phi.
